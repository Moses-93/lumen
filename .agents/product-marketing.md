**Document version:** v1

## Product Overview

**One-liner:** A private, local-first AI journaling system that acts as a digital mirror to your thoughts.
**What it does:** Lumen allows users to input their unfiltered, chaotic thoughts. Instead of offering generative advice, it returns a single, semantically resonant quote to provide grounding and clarity.
**Product category:** Mental Health / Journaling App
**Product type:** Mobile Application (B2C)
**Business model:** Freemium (Core loop free, microtransactions for specialized quote databases via Apple In-App Purchases)

## Problems & Pain Points

**Core problem:** Overthinking, emotional overwhelm, and the lack of a secure, non-judgmental space to process chaotic thoughts.
**Why alternatives fall short:**

- Traditional diaries require too much effort and give no feedback.
- Generative AI (like ChatGPT) gives unsolicited, generic "advice" that feels artificial and invasive.
- Cloud-based journals pose massive privacy risks for highly sensitive data.
**What it costs them:** Peace of mind, focus, and time lost to mental loops.
**Emotional tension:** High anxiety, fear of judgment, data privacy paranoia.

## Competitive Landscape

**Direct:** Day One, Journey — fall short because they offer no reflection or feedback (purely passive).
**Secondary:** ChatGPT, Pi — fall short because they give generic "advice" and store data on servers (privacy risk).
**Indirect:** Traditional paper journal — falls short because it's hard to search and provides no contextual insight.

## Differentiation

**Key differentiators:**

- 100% Local Inference (Zero data leaves the device).
- "Living Response" (Provides a resonant quote, not AI-generated advice).
- Anti-AI Positioning (It's a mirror, not a mentor).
**How we do it differently:** We use on-device ONNX models to find semantic similarity between a user's thought and a curated database of philosophical/psychological quotes.
**Why that's better:** Unmatched privacy and profound, human-centric wisdom rather than robotic text generation.
**Why customers choose us:** They want the benefits of AI analysis without the privacy risks or the patronizing tone of a chatbot.

## Objections

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "Will my data be used to train AI?" | "No. Everything happens directly on your phone's processor. We literally can't see your data." |
| "Why pay for quotes I can google?" | "You're paying for the precise timing and semantic matching—getting the exact wisdom you need at the exact second you need it." |

**Anti-persona:** People looking for an AI therapist to tell them what to do; people who want to share their journals publicly.

## Switching Dynamics

**Push:** Exhaustion from overthinking; privacy concerns with existing AI tools.
**Pull:** The promise of instant clarity and 100% local privacy.
**Habit:** Opening Notes app or physical diary.
**Anxiety:** Fear of losing data if the phone breaks (need local backup strategy).
